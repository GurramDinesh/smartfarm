About Us

About SmartFarm

Addressing grassroots agricultural hurdles with accessible digital tools.

The Problem We Solve

Over 80% of small and marginal farmers make crop choices based on guesswork or informal hearsay. Inappropriate soil-crop matching leads to crop failures, ground water exhaustion, and severe yield reductions. Furthermore, delays in identifying early fungal and pest diseases result in heavy financial losses.

Our Hackathon Solution

SmartFarm is a lightweight, zero-dependency, bilingual web guide that provides immediate, rule-based crop guidance, disease mitigation strategies, and practical water conservation methods without requiring costly hardware, paid API keys, or internet bandwidth hurdles.
